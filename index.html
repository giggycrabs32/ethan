What We Do

Comprehensive Security Solutions

From cash-in-transit to sophisticated vault management, Brink's Canada delivers end-to-end security services built for businesses of every size.

Cash Management

Complete cash lifecycle solutions — counting, sorting, reporting, and deposit preparation with full audit trails.

  • Automated cash counting
  • Deposit preparation & reconciliation
  • Currency management reporting
  • Coin & note processing

Secure Transport

Armoured vehicle services with trained, uniformed guards ensuring safe delivery of currency, valuables, and sensitive documents.

  • Cash-in-transit (CIT) services
  • High-value cargo transport
  • Scheduled & on-demand pickup
  • GPS-tracked armoured vehicles

ATM Services

Full-service ATM management — from cash loading and first-line maintenance to monitoring and emergency response.

  • ATM cash replenishment
  • First and second line maintenance
  • 24/7 ATM monitoring
  • Network & software support

Vault Services

Secure vault storage and management for banks, retailers, and enterprises requiring controlled access and accountability.

  • Managed vault outsourcing
  • Dual-control vault access
  • Inventory tracking & audits
  • Safe & vault installation

About Us

Newfoundland's Most Trusted Security Partner

Brink's Canada Ltd in Mount Pearl, NL, is part of the global Brink's Company — the world's leading provider of cash management and secure logistics. Our local team brings the same rigorous standards and proven expertise to every client engagement in Atlantic Canada.

We serve financial institutions, retailers, healthcare facilities, government agencies, and enterprises requiring the highest level of physical security and cash handling precision.

  • Fully licensed, bonded, and insured security professionals
  • Advanced GPS tracking and real-time fleet monitoring
  • Rigorous background checks and ongoing employee training
  • 24/7 operations center with immediate incident response
