Make sure it rings

Before you go to sleep
  1. Open WakeScan and leave it on screen (turn on Keep screen awake above).
  2. Plug your phone in to charge.
  3. Turn media volume up — the alarm plays through your phone's speaker.
  4. That's it. In the morning the only way out is to get up and scan.

Heads up: because this is a web app, it can't wake your phone from a locked screen like the built-in clock app can. Keeping the app open overnight is what makes it reliable.

Setting up your items
  1. Go to the Items tab and add an item, e.g. "Bathroom sink".
  2. Print its QR sticker (or just draw the code's screen up on another device) and stick it on the item.
  3. Or pick something that already has a barcode — toothpaste, shampoo, a cereal box — and link its existing code.
  4. In each alarm, tick the items you want to be forced to scan. Untick to remove them.
Camera & install tips
  • Scanning needs camera permission — your browser will ask the first time.
  • The page must be served over https:// for the camera to work.
  • Add WakeScan to your home screen (browser menu → "Add to Home Screen") so it opens full screen like a real app.
  • Dark room? Tap the flash button in the scanner.
